New Patient Inquiries Approach

Approach

A comprehensive, whole-body approach to musculoskeletal care.

Following an injury, the body develops compensations that change how it moves. Over time, these patterns overload the body and perpetuate pain. Treatment begins by identifying them and correcting the underlying mechanics.

Find the cause

A full-body examination establishes the mechanism of injury and identifies any compensations the body has adopted as a result.

Release the tension

Fascia is connective tissue that links the whole body. Fascial restrictions hold tension, limit movement and create pain. Hands-on fascial work releases that tension.

Make it last

Proper mechanics, alignment and symmetry take unnecessary strain off the whole system. You move better and, more importantly, you stay better.

About

Christine Meizoso, DPT

With a career spanning more than twenty years, Christine has treated thousands of patients in orthopedic and sports rehab, from weekend warriors to professional athletes. She began working at Columbia University Medical Center with athletes whose livelihoods depended on getting treatment right. After directing an outpatient orthopedic clinic from 2010 to 2014, she opened her own practice in 2014, built around one-on-one care.

The initial session starts with a full-body exam to find the mechanism of injury and any compensations hiding beneath the pain. Every one-on-one session is built around skilled, hands-on fascial work and soft and deep tissue mobilization to release tension and take stress off an overworked system.

She also designs individualized strength and running programs around each patient’s short-term and long-term goals. For athletes and teams, she builds preseason and in-season programs that reduce injury risk and improve efficiency and performance. On-site ergonomic consultations at the workplace are also available as an additional service.

The goal is to get you back, and keep you there.
